OmniTune User Guide & Instructions

Everything you need to master multi-instrument comparison, alternate tunings, scale degrees, harmonica bending, and guitar chords.

1. OmniTune: Instrument Comparison & Scales

OmniTune allows you to visualize and cross-compare pitches, scale patterns, and arpeggios simultaneously across multiple instruments.

4 Universal Instrument Banks

Every instrument bank (Banks 1 through 4) can load any instrument in the catalog:

  • 10-Hole Diatonic Harmonicas: Multiple specialized tunings (Standard Richter, Wilde Tuning, Paddy Richter, Dorian, PowerBender, Diminished, Augmented, Whole Tone, etc.) in major and minor keys, with optional Half-Valving.
  • Chromatic Harmonicas: 12-hole and 16-hole models across all 12 keys with Solo, Bebop, C6, and PowerChromatic configurations.
  • 6-String Guitar: Standard, Drop D, DADGAD, Open C, Open E, Open G, Open D, etc. spanning 24 frets.
  • 4-String Ukulele: Standard Soprano C (GCEA), Baritone, Low G, and D-Tuning spanning 15 frets.
  • Piano Keyboard: Multi-octave chromatic keyboard laid out with white and black key rows.

Interactive Note Highlighting & Sound

  • Mouse Hover / Sweep: Hover over any note to automatically highlight all enharmonically equivalent notes across all active instrument banks.
  • Audio Playback: Click or click-and-drag across notes to synthesize pitches in real-time. Use the Pluck (decaying pluck) and Synth (sustained tone) buttons in the persistent top bar to toggle tone styles.

Scales, Chords & Scale Degree Display

  • Display Scale: Choose any Key (C, D, E, F#, etc.) and Scale/Chord (Major, Minor, Blues, Pentatonic, Dorian, Dominant 7th, Major 9th, etc.) and click Display Scale. All notes in the scale light up across all instruments, with the Root notes highlighted in bold red.
  • Show Scale Degrees: Toggles the display of Nashville Number System scale degrees (1, b3, 3, 4, b5, 5, b7, 7, etc.) positioned above each note in high-contrast white text with black borders.
  • Show Note Names: Displays letter names (C, D, E, F#, etc.) with a crisp white text outline for readability over black piano keys and dark frets.
  • Accidentals Toggle (Notes: ♯ / ♭): Switch note names between Sharps (C#, D#, F#, G#, A#) and Flats (Db, Eb, Gb, Ab, Bb). The diagram instantly updates all note labels across all active instruments.

Printing Instrument Diagrams

  • Print Diagram Button (🖨️ Print): Each instrument bank header includes a dedicated Print button. Clicking it formats that specific instrument diagram for paper printing in landscape orientation, with auto-scaling to fit the page and a metadata banner displaying the instrument, tuning, active scale, and view settings.
  • Respects Current Settings: Printouts accurately reflect your current display configuration—including whether Note Names or Scale Degrees are shown or hidden, and any highlighted scale degrees and root notes.
  • Full Comparison Print: Using standard browser print (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) prints all active loaded instruments cleanly without page chrome or controls.

2. Harmonica Notation & Bending Guide

OmniTune dynamically calculates blow, draw, bend, overblow, overdraw, and valve-bend possibilities for any harmonica key and tuning.

Badge / Notation Technique Description & Example
1 to 10 Blow Reed Standard natural blow reed on that hole (e.g. Hole 4 Blow = C4 on a C Richter).
-1 to -10 Draw Reed Standard natural draw reed on that hole (e.g. Hole 4 Draw = D4 on a C Richter).
DB (-3', -3'', -3''') Draw Bend Dual-reed draw bend pulling pitch down (each apostrophe represents one semitone).
BB (8', 9') Blow Bend Dual-reed blow bend on higher holes where the blow reed is higher in pitch than the draw reed.
OB (6+ / 6ob) Overblow Embouchure technique to block the blow reed in order to vibrate the draw reed at a higher pitch (e.g. Hole 6 Overblow = Bb4).
OD (-7od) Overdraw Embouchure technique to block the draw reed to vibrate the blow reed at a higher pitch (e.g. Hole 7 Overdraw = C#5).
VB (v) Half-Valved Bend Single-reed valve bend enabled when valves are installed to block air passage from the opposing slot.
< (<1, <-1) Chromatic Slide-In Pushing the slide button in on a Chromatic harmonica to access the sharp/flat reed plates.

3. Progression Studio & Guide Tone Visualizer

Accessible via progressionstudio.html, the Progression Studio combines a real-time Web Audio backing band (chords, bass, and drums) with a synchronized multi-instrument guide-tone visualizer for practicing improvisation, voice leading, and chord changes across any genre.

🎓 Interactive Tutorial Mode & Practice Setups

  • Settings Tour: Click 🎓 Tutorial Mode in the top header to launch a non-blocking, 8-step walkthrough that spotlights each section of the studio while keeping every control live and interactive.
  • One-Click Practice Setups: Switch to the 🎯 Practice Setups tab to instantly configure curated drills—such as Target the 3rds, Voice Leading Between Two Chords (with ii7 → V7, I7 → IV7, and im7 → IV7 pair options), Overlay a Scale Over Chord Changes, Modal Rock Triads, Color Tones (No Roots), Bass & Drums Only, and Dual-Instrument Translation. Your primary instrument & tuning are preserved, and ↩ Restore Setup lets you revert to your prior progression anytime.

🔊 Backing Band, Rhythm & Sound Control Panel

  • Chord Instrument / Sound: Switch chord playback between Electric Piano (Rhodes), Acoustic Grand Piano, Warm Organ / Synth Pad, Jazz Archtop Clean Guitar, Acoustic / Crystal Clean Guitar, Blues Tube Overdrive Guitar, Rock Crunch Guitar, High-Gain Lead, or Mute Chords (for practicing over bass and drums alone).
  • Comping / Rhythm Style: Choose how chords are articulated in each bar, including Sustained Whole-Bar Pad, Syncopated Comp (Charleston), Jazz Swing, Bossa Nova, Blues Shuffle, Arpeggiated, Funk / Soul 16th Groove, Folk / Pop Strum, or Steady Quarter-Note Pulse.
  • Bassline Mode: Select Walking Bass (1–3–5–Chromatic Approach), Root Sustained, Root–5th Alternating (Two-Feel / Bossa), 8th-Note Groove, Bass Only, or Bass Off.
  • Beat / Drum Groove: Play along with synthesized Jazz Swing Ride & Hi-Hat, Blues 12/8 Shuffle, Bossa Nova / Rimshot, Funk / Neo-Soul Pocket, Pop / Rock 8th Groove, Half-Time Ballad, Metronome Click Only, or Drums Off.
  • Voicing, Tempo, Drive & Mix: Filter chord playback to Full Chord, Shell Voicing (R–3–7), Triad, Power 5th, or Bass Note Only; dial in any tempo from 40 to 220 BPM; adjust tube saturation with the Drive slider; and balance the Chords / Bass / Drums volume sliders in real time.

🎼 Chord Selector & Progression Builder

  • Song Key Selector: Click any of the 12 Song Key pills (C through B) to transpose preset progressions, Roman numeral pads, and background scale overlays immediately.
  • Load a Preset Progression: Choose from dozens of curated progressions grouped by genre (Blues, Rock, Jazz, R&B / Neo-Soul, and Pop / Folk)—including 12-Bar Blues, Minor Blues, Classic Rock Anthems, Andalusian Cadences, Jazz ii–V–I Turnarounds, and Rhythm Changes. Loading a preset also configures an idiomatic backing band style and tempo.
  • Build & Add an Individual Chord: Pick any Chord Root Note, any of the 21 OmniTune Chord Qualities (triads, power chords, 7ths, 6ths, and extended 9th chords), an Inversion (Root, 1st, 2nd, or 3rd inversion), and a Bar Length (½ Bar, 1 Bar, or 2 Bars). Click 🔊 Hear to preview the chord or + Add to append it to your timeline.
  • Quick Roman Numeral Palette: Click the collapsible 🎹 Quick Roman Numeral Palette bar to expand diatonic and borrowed modal chord pads in the active key. Tapping any pad plays the chord and appends it to the timeline.

⛓️ Active Chord Progression Timeline & Transport Controls

  • Playback & Loop Controls: Use ▶ Play Progression (or ⏹ Stop Playing to halt audio immediately), toggle Loop, click 🔔 Test Sound to audition the currently selected chord, or click 🗑 Clear Progression to start a fresh timeline from scratch.
  • Timeline Editing & Durations: Click any chord pill in the timeline to jump to and audition that bar. Use the per-chord duration dropdown (½b, 1b, 2b) to adjust how many beats the chord lasts (a ½b chord plays for 2 beats at the current tempo, 1b plays for 4 beats, and 2b plays for 8 beats), or click ✕ on any chord pill to remove it.
  • Stepping & Pinning: Use ◀ Prev Chord and Next Chord ▶ to step through changes manually while practicing, or click 📌 Pin Base to lock a reference chord on the timeline.

🎯 Real-Time Multi-Instrument Guide Tone Visualizer

  • Multi-Instrument & Dual Comparison: Visualize chord changes on Guitar (all alternate tunings, 24 frets), Ukulele & 4-String Bass, Piano Keyboard (4 octaves), 10-Hole Diatonic Harmonicas (Richter, Wilde Tuned, Wilde Minor, Dom's Shred Minor, Natural Minor, Paddy Richter, PowerBender, etc., with optional Valved bends), and 12/16-Hole Chromatic Harmonicas. Click + Compare 2nd Instrument to display a second instrument diagram simultaneously.
  • Target Presets & Granular Degree Pills: Filter the diagram to show Guide Tones + Root (R, 3rd, 7th), Guide Tones Only (3rd & 7th), All Active Chord Tones, Triad Tones, or toggle individual harmonic roles (R, 3rd/♭3, 7th/♭7, 5th/♭5, 6th/13th, 9th/2nd).
  • Next Chord Ghosting (👻 Next Ghost): When enabled, the upcoming chord's guide tones and root appear as dashed ghost rings on the diagram (with the last chord wrapping around to preview the first chord), helping you spot smooth voice-leading paths before the next bar arrives.
  • Scale Overlay & Interactive Note Inspector: Select a background Scale Overlay (Blues, Minor/Major Pentatonic, Dorian, Mixolydian, Major, Natural/Harmonic/Melodic Minor, Bebop, Lydian) to display scale tones as violet dots behind the active chord tones. Tap any fret, harmonica hole/bend, or piano key to hear its pitch and inspect its exact harmonic role in the bottom inspector bar.

4. Guitar Chord Progression Visualizer

Accessible via guitarprogression.html, this tool provides an interactive 24-fret guitar neck for finding chord voicings, visualizing scale patterns, and mapping chord progressions on both desktop and mobile devices.

  • Tuning Selector: Explore Standard (EADGBE), Eb Standard, Perfect Fourths (EADGCF), Perfect Fourths Eb (EbAbDbGbBE), Drop D, DADGAD, Open C, Open E, BEBEBE, Open G, and Open D.
  • Chord & Scale Modes: Map chords with standard or shell voicings, or overlay Major, Minor, Pentatonic, Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Harmonic Minor, and Melodic Minor scales across 24 frets.
  • Progression Builder & Multi-Chord Layering: Add up to 6 chords or scales to a progression sequence. Non-highlighted chords render in translucent background color while active chords render brightly.
  • Mobile-Friendly Audition & Pinning:
    • Pin Base Chord (📌): Pin any chord or scale as a persistent baseline anchor on the fretboard.
    • Audition / Hover Preview (👁️): Tap any chord pill or use the mobile step buttons (◀ Prev / Next ▶) to preview/hover that chord and inspect its scale degrees overlaid against the pinned base chord.
    • Fret Inspector: Tap any fret or string on the fretboard to view harmonic degrees and note details in real time.
    • Swipeable 24-Fret Neck: Horizontally scroll the fretboard smoothly on mobile phones without cramped or distorted note circles.
  • Toggle Overlays: Show or hide note names and Nashville scale degrees on the fretboard.

5. Chord Chart Generator (Box Diagrams)

Accessible via chordchartgenerator.html, this tool generates publication-quality chord box diagrams with PNG export capabilities.

  • Configurable Diagram: Customize fret count (1–24), string count (1–12), starting fret position, muted strings (X), open strings (O), and finger numbers.
  • High-Resolution PNG Export: Click Save as PNG to export clean, white-background diagram images.
  • URL Query Parameter Driven: Supports programmatic creation and LLM automation via URL parameters (e.g. ?chord=G&strings=6&frets=5&s0_fret=3...).

6. Offline & Client-Side Architecture

OmniTune is 100% self-contained and client-side:

  • Zero Server Dependencies: Runs directly in any web browser without Node.js servers, databases, or cloud backends.
  • Offline Ready: Clone or download the repository to use it completely offline on your desktop, laptop, or mobile browser.
  • Web Audio API: Dynamic sound synthesis produces responsive audio across all modern browsers.
